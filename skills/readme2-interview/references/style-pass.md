# Style pass (mandatory before any prose output)

Every article, post or summary shown to the interviewee goes through this pass.

## 1. Use a humanizer skill when one is installed

If a humanizer skill is available in the session (for example `humanizer:humanizer`),
load it and apply it to the draft in embedded mode. It must keep every fact, number,
date and quote unchanged.

## 2. Otherwise, apply this checklist yourself

Condensed from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" (WikiProject AI Cleanup):

- No "not X but Y" contrasts that add weight without adding a claim.
- No one-line closers that repeat the paragraph ("That is the real win.").
- No sayings that sound deep ("at its core", "the real question is").
- No staged openers ("Let's dive in", "Here's the thing").
- No forced groups of three; keep three only when there are three things.
- No dashes as universal connectors; use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
- No inflated significance ("pivotal", "a testament to", "game-changer").
- No sales language ("seamless", "robust", "cutting-edge").
- No bold labels on every list item.
- Simple verbs: "is", "has", not "serves as", "boasts".
- Vary sentence length. Keep the interviewee's own odd, specific details.

## 3. Check after the pass

- No fact, number, date, name or quote added or lost.
- Quotes are still word for word.
- Set `humanizer: done` in the article frontmatter.
